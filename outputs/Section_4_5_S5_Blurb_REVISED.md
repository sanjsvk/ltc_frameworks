# Section 4.5 S5 Weak Signal – Revised Blurb

LTC signal collapses to 14.3% of S1 baseline ($0.176M vs $1.229M/week)—an 85.7% reduction that pushes LTC below the noise floor. All 10 models return 0% recovery with frozen S1 parameters, demonstrating that signal strength, not architecture, determines identifiability below an empirical threshold. This universal collapse is a **calibration boundary, not a structural limitation**: supplementary optimization reveals that MCMC achieves 88.5% recovery when re-calibrated with scenario-specific priors (weaker decay assumptions, tighter coefficient constraints), proving the 0% is calibration-dependent. Conversely, fixed-parameter models (F1 static adstocks, F2 distributed lags, deterministic F3 methods like Kalman DLM and BSTS) remain at 0% even with aggressive re-tuning, confirming that **joint Bayesian optimization over the latent stock structure is structurally essential** below the signal threshold. The +88.5pp calibration gap uniquely available to MCMC represents the maximum achievable improvement and applies exclusively to the model whose parameterisation matches the data-generating process.
