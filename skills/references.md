## Format (JMR / AMA Style)

**Journal article:**
Last, First (Year), "Title of Article," *Journal Name*, Volume (Issue), pages.

Clarke, Darral G. (1976), "Econometric Measurement of the Duration of Advertising
Effect on Sales," *Journal of Marketing Research*, 13 (4), 345–357.

**Book:**
Last, First (Year), *Title of Book*, City: Publisher.

Harvey, Andrew C. (1990), *Forecasting, Structural Time Series Models and the
Kalman Filter*, Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.

**Working paper / preprint:**
Last, First (Year), "Title," working paper, Institution.

---

## In-Text Citation Format

One author: (Clarke 1976)
Two authors: (Srinivasan and Hanssens 2009)
Three or more: (Jin et al. 2017)
Multiple citations in one parenthetical — alphabetical order:
(Clarke 1976; Hanssens et al. 2001; Koyck 1954)

No comma between author and year in JMR style.

---

## Core References to Include (Minimum)

**Adstock foundations:**
- Clarke (1976) — duration of advertising effects
- Broadbent (1979) — advertising budget setting
- Koyck (1954) — geometric distributed lag

**MMM methods:**
- Hanssens, Parsons, and Schultz (2001) — Market Response Models
- Jin et al. (2017) — Bayesian MMM
- Robyn paper (Meta, 2021) if citing open-source MMM tools

**State-space / time series:**
- Harvey (1990) — structural time series
- Durbin and Koopman (2012) — time series analysis by state space methods

**Brand equity:**
- Keller (1993) — conceptualising brand equity
- Srinivasan and Hanssens (2009) — marketing and firm value

**Distributed lag:**
- Almon (1965) — polynomial distributed lag
- Weibull papers for MMM lag shapes

## Completeness Check

Before finalising:
- Every in-text citation appears in the reference list
- Every reference in the list is cited in the text
- All journal names are italicised
- All article titles are in quotation marks
- Volume and issue numbers are present for all journal articles
- Page ranges are complete
